# Security policy

Deposplit splits secrets among people you choose, so a vulnerability here can cost somebody
their password manager, their wallet, or their disk. Reports are welcome and taken
seriously.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please report privately through **GitHub's private vulnerability reporting** — the
*Security* tab of this repository, then *Report a vulnerability*. Please do not open a
public issue for a security problem.

Useful things to include: the commit, what an attacker gains, and the smallest reproduction
you have.

## Scope and policy

The full policy, the threat model, and the list of already-known and accepted limitations
live in the hub repository:

- [SECURITY.md](https://github.com/Deposplit/deposplit.com/blob/main/SECURITY.md)
- [docs/security.md](https://github.com/Deposplit/deposplit.com/blob/main/docs/security.md)
- [docs/trust-model.md](https://github.com/Deposplit/deposplit.com/blob/main/docs/trust-model.md)

Deposplit is **pre-launch**: nothing is published to an app store and no production relay
serves real users. Reports against `main` are in scope; there are no released versions.
